About Us

Learn about our history, vision, mission, and the faculty who teach here.

Institute History

Established in 1998, CVR College of Engineering has grown from a small technical institute into a leading center for engineering education. Over the years, the college has expanded its academic programs, strengthened industry partnerships, and invested in modern infrastructure to support teaching, research, and student development.

Vision

To be a center of excellence in engineering education and research, empowering students to become innovative, ethical, and socially responsible leaders.

Mission

  • Deliver high-quality, industry-relevant education.
  • Foster research, innovation, and entrepreneurship.
  • Promote holistic development and ethical values.
  • Engage with industry and community to solve real-world problems.
